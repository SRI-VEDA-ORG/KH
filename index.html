About Us

About Krishna Children's & Dental Hospital

Located in the heart of Allagadda, Krishna Children's and Dental Hospital is a trusted healthcare center dedicated to the well-being of families. We focus primarily on comprehensive children's healthcare and professional dental care in a warm, welcoming environment.

Operating across a well-organized three-floor facility, we provide daily checkups, pediatric consultations, oral health care, and medicine support. With over 20 beds available, we ensure comfortable observation and inpatient care when your little ones need it most.

"A caring healthcare place for children and families."
