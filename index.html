About Krishna Children's & Dental Hospital

Conveniently situated right opposite the RTC Bus Depot on Kadapa Road, Allagadda, Krishna Children's and Dental Hospital has been serving the regional community with clinical excellence and warmth for over 30 years.

Our multi-specialty center combines specialized Pediatric Care for newborn infants and growing children with an advanced Dental Surgical Facility. Equipped with a 30-bed inpatient ward, Level-2 Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU), and in-house diagnostic laboratory, we are dedicated to providing accessible, top-tier healthcare.
